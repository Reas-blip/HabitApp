SCENARIO 2:
5 Star Review
SwiftDesk is a virtual assistant service that, as a business owner, I found to be very good. Before finding a virtual assistant service, I did not expect anything special, but using this service surpassed my expectations. In my company, our emails, meetings, and calendars were not synced. This led to a lot of inefficiencies wasting both time and money.  After subscribing to this service, I immediately saw an increase in efficiency of our operations, particularly in how quickly our team could process requests.
Managing the meetings, emails, and calendars lightened the administrative load on our staff,
allowing them to focus more on core operations. I would recommend this service, as you will earn back the money spent on it.

3 Star Review
My experience with this service is balanced. As a business owner, I understand how important it is to organize our meetings, emails and calendars. This service improved our core operations allowing us to reach more customers. However, it needs improvement. As an IT call center, we need a virtual assistant service, but the response times during busy hours are quite slow, which affects our operations as the busy hours are usually the most crucial.
Overall, the service offered is good, but I believe the response times can be improved.
